# Mortgage Intelligence | B2B partnership workspace
Prepared: 14 October 2026

## Honest product status
Research beta on a draft pull request, NOT bank-certified, FCA-authorised, financially advised, production-hosted, or commercially validated. Existing functionality: mortgage repayment and rate-shock calculations, a 13-month UK quoted mortgage market index snapshot, an experimental Bank Rate outlook, and a synthetic three-loan professional demo. API contract in FastAPI OpenAPI during local development.

## Buyer positioning
Start with independent mortgage brokers, small broker networks and mortgage software providers. Sell a workflow: reproducible hypothetical scenarios and compact market context. Avoid selling unvalidated interest-rate prediction accuracy or lender eligibility claims.

For larger banks, position only as a **synthetic-data proof-of-concept enquiry**, not a proposal to replace core lending or underwriting platforms. Procurement normally includes technical, information-security, data governance and contracting reviews.

## Commercial hypotheses (NOT price offers)
- Broker seat / office licence: to be validated through interviews and manual pilots
- Professional white-label module: negotiated setup + support, only after secure multi-tenancy
- Bank PoC: negotiated fixed-scope engagement with agreed outputs, no promise of procurement
