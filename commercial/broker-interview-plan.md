# UK broker customer discovery and pilot qualification

## First-customer hypothesis
Small independent brokers who repeatedly explain rate shocks and repayment sensitivity to clients may value auditable visual comparisons. This is a hypothesis, not validated demand.

## Qualify before pitching
- Does the firm already use CRM/illustrations/ESIS or other compliance-approved tools?
- Which step takes time or generates confusing repeat questions?
- Do clients need generic scenarios or regulated product-specific advice?
- Who owns tool approval and purchase decisions?
- What evidence and information-security requirements block outside vendors?

## Eight interview questions (avoid leading the witness)
1. Walk me through the last mortgage rate-scenario explanation you gave.
2. What tools did you use, and what had to be copied between them?
3. Where do mistakes or rework happen most often?
4. What was the actual consequence when this workflow went badly?
5. Which features are mandatory to remain compliant with your firm's process?
6. Would hypothetical scenarios help if they carried no live lender quotes?
7. Who would approve a new tool, and how do you measure success?
8. Would you trial a synthetic-data demo; what would make it not useful?

## Prospecting approach
Speak with 5-10 relevant brokers/networks, seeking permission for a short discovery interview. No scraped customer records, no mass automated unsolicited emails, and no claims of licensing or FCA affiliation. Verify recipient details independently.

## Broker outreach draft (UNSENT)
Subject: Could I understand your mortgage scenario workflow?

Hello,

I'm building an early-stage UK mortgage planning tool, and I'd value a short conversation about how your team handles repayment illustrations and hypothetical interest-rate scenarios.

This isn't a lender-rate feed or mortgage advice tool. I'm looking to understand whether there is a genuine workflow problem worth solving before proposing a controlled synthetic-data pilot.

Would you be open to a brief call? I'm not requesting any customer records.

Kind regards,
[Name and verified contact details]

## Decision criterion
Continue only if multiple independent participants report the same costly workflow and at least one wants a measured pilot. Collect consented product feedback, not borrower PII. Record objections as seriously as positive answers.
